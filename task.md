## Design Context

### Users
Prospective and current patients, often visiting from a phone while deciding whether to contact a clinic. They need clear care information, an honest view of this fictional demo, and an easy next step.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, and considerate. The experience should build confidence through plain language and transparent demo labeling, not unsupported claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing clinical-teal identity on a cool, bright ground. Use expressive typography, generous spacing, and real clinic imagery; keep the interface precise and avoid decorative effects or invented credibility signals.

### Design Principles
1. Make the clinic and the next action clear in the first viewport.
2. Keep the primary action easy to find on touch and keyboard.
3. Use motion sparingly to support the care story, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
4. Keep fictional clinic details visibly identified as demo content.
5. Prioritize readable hierarchy and a considered responsive layout over extra decoration.
